THE CORE PHILOSOPHY

Mindful Reminders • Respectful Serenity

The Adhan is a sacred, universally revered spiritual summons. In Muslim-majority lands, it echoes majestically across minarets. Yet on personal mobile devices, unexpected high-volume alarms often create unintended challenges.

🏢
Quiet Workplaces & Meetings
In corporate boardrooms, open offices, classrooms, or libraries, a sudden blaring mobile alarm can startle colleagues and create awkward disruptions.
🕌
Inside the Mosque & Congregation
If an unsilenced mobile phone rings loudly during congregational prayer, it unintentionally disturbs worshippers and cuts through the solemn focus.
🌍
Living in Multicultural Societies
For Muslims living in Western or non-Muslim majority nations, unfamiliar loud alarms on public transit or in public venues can cause awkward tension or anxiety. Sakina offers a peaceful, dignified personal reminder.
INNOVATION & CRAFT

Built for Elegance and Peace of Mind

Every detail was designed with purpose, blending historical Islamic astronomy with the pinnacle of modern mobile engineering.

🧭

3D Astrolabe Horology

A bespoke central 3D rotating dial inspired by Andalusian astrolabes, featuring a solitary golden hand with realistic drop shadow, domed sapphire crystal reflection, and a balanced 3-above / 3-below zero-scroll card layout.

🌿

Gentle Nature Chimes (1x - 3x)

Replaces loud sirens with calming, peaceful nature soundscapes: dawn breeze, morning birdsong, or crystal dew. Choose a single polite chime or repeat up to 3 gentle times.

🔋

0% Background Battery Drain

Zero persistent background services, zero continuous wakelocks. Wakes your device only at the exact prayer millisecond using native Android AlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle.

🛡️

100% Offline & Private

Calculates prayer times mathematically on-device using high-precision Jean Meeus astronomical algorithms. Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero data sent over the network.

🎨

Cupertino Frosted Glass UI

Award-winning iOS-style translucent glass aesthetic with rich themes: Rawdah Emerald Green and Midnight Sky Blue, rendering at a fluid 120Hz.

🌐

Universal Coordinate Geometry

Clock dial stations remain firmly anchored in fixed astronomical positions across all languages, while center information dynamically adapts to Arabic RTL and system locales.
